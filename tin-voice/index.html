Testing it
  1. Connect your Bluetooth speaker first, then tap Start and allow the mic.
  2. Keep the speaker a few steps away with the volume low, then turn it up slowly.
  3. Raise the Gate until the room noise stops buzzing but your voice still comes through.
  4. Keep this screen open. iPhone stops the mic when Safari goes to the background.
